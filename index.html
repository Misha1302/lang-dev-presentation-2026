Appendix · DI / builder

Why isn't this just Dependency Injection?

Builder / DI

Excellent when the host already knows what to instantiate and wire.

Planning

Adds an explicit phase for whole-language dependencies, conflicts, provider ambiguity, ordering and artifact routes.

Boundary

UT can still use ordinary objects and DI-like construction after the global decisions are resolved.

The distinction is ownership of global composition decisions, not “DI is bad”.

Appendix · prior art

Why not LLVM / MLIR / a language workbench?

LLVM

Excellent compiler infrastructure once the relevant IR/toolchain composition is already defined.

MLIR

Extensible multi-level IR with dialects, operations, types and conversion infrastructure.

Racket / MPS

Richer language-oriented ecosystems and workbench models at different layers.

This talk

Whole runnable-language composition → explicit plan/runtime boundary, plus local rewrite evidence.

Positioning only. No novelty or superiority claim.

Appendix · deterministic routing

Deterministic selection is a protocol property, not semantic equivalence

A route can be deterministically selected from expressed contracts and planning cost while two implementations still differ semantically.

Equal-cost/tie behavior and provider preferences belong to the planning protocol and should be tested as such. Route Cost is not execution-time performance.
