Appendix A3 · Fail closed

What makes planning reject a language?

Dependencyrequired contribution was explicitly excluded
Ownershipsingle-owner slot has an unresolved collision
Capabilityno provider — or multiple providers without preference
Artifact routeno typed route reaches the backend's exact input contract
Executorambiguous exact backend/input match

A broken composition should fail before runtime materialization.
