After the attack

The narrowed research hypothesis

Can a language feature depend on semantic capabilities rather than one concrete type-system implementation, be globally resolved with independently authored typing components, and then be specialized once the concrete language and program establish the required legality conditions?

Current witness boundary

Implemented / partial witness

Declarative profiles, staged planning, snapshotted plans, selected backend parity, feasibility before preference, bounded local deabstraction.

Target / hypothesis

Delegates across independently authored type semantics and full shared callable capability contracts.
